Simulate a URL

Runs the provider's entries on a URL in the order above, one step at a time, pass after pass while the URL changes. It's a model of the order, not the engine: the provider's patterns and exceptions, $removeparam modifiers and preprocessors aren't checked. The custom rules editor in Linkumori simulates with the engine itself.
