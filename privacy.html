Definitions

"GitHub" means GitHub, Inc. and its parent companies, subsidiaries, affiliates, and any successors or assigns, together with any service provider, platform, or entity that hosts, operates, or takes over the GitHub Pages service or this site's hosting.

"We", "us", and "our" mean the owner of this site.

What we collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. This site uses no cookies, analytics, or trackers.

Information kept in your browser

Some pages save small preferences in your browser's local storage, such as the light or dark theme and the list addresses you type into the filter list converter. They stay on your device and are never sent to us. You can remove them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Filter list converter and other sites

The filter list converter can fetch a filter list from an address you enter. When you press Fetch or Fetch all, or a list you load uses !#include, your browser connects directly to the site at that address. That site, not us, receives your IP address and whatever else your browser sends with the request, and handles it under its own privacy policy. We do not receive, see, or store the request, the address, or the list you fetch.

We do not choose, check, or control the addresses you enter or the sites behind them, and we are not responsible for how those sites collect, use, or share information. Only fetch lists from sites you trust. If you paste a list or load it from a file instead, nothing leaves your browser.

Hosting by GitHub

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect your IP address and any other information from your visit, including device, browser, and usage data, and any data you submit to or through GitHub. We do not receive this information, do not know what is collected, and have no control over how GitHub uses, stores, or shares it. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

Changes to this policy

We reserve the right to change this policy at any time, for any reason, without prior notice. Changes take effect when posted on this page. Your continued use of this site means you accept the updated policy.
